Released rough, on purpose

Honesty first: the intro is a little rough and the ending is a little rough. The middle 90% — the build, the drop, we party till we win, we occupy with love — is exactly where it should be.

If you're a DJ, you were never going to play my intro anyway. You're going to mix in, loop your favourite moment, and mix out. So instead of sitting on the track for another month polishing thirty seconds nobody hears in the club, it's out now. Perfecting an intro that gets blended away is a bad trade against a dance floor tonight.

When the final master lands, it lands here first. Same page, same links.
