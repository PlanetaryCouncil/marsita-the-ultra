# Sahara Elongated City

**An Elon-gated community.** One city, the entire desert — from the northern edge of the Sahara to the southern edge, and every grain of sand in between.

A satirical brochure website with a real payload.

## The joke

- A gated city 4,800 km long, with exactly one gate (it's a really good gate)
- Every grain of sand is a spaceship that hasn't been born yet (*Dune & Done*)
- The Machine: harvests sand, 3D-prints one home per day. Not two. We asked.
- 13 months × 28 days, the month of Sol, Year Day (International Fixed Calendar — Kodak really used it until 1989)
- Official language: Esperanto. *Unu urbo. Unu lingvo. Unu pordego.*
- Taxes paid in Vibes and Karma, audited by the Ministry of Vibes
- Vibex® — *"Vibes good must be."*

## The real part

Buried inside the ridiculous brochure is a genuine thesis about water restoration:

- **Refill, baby, refill** — managed aquifer recharge instead of extraction
- **[The Qattara Depression Project](qattara.html)** — a real Egyptian basin 133 m below sea level; seawater hydro proposed since 1912 (the 1970s plan involved 213 nukes; Egypt said no). First step either way: **demine El Alamein**.
- **[The Lake Chad Refill](lake-chad.html)** — ~30 million people depend on a lake that's ~90% gone; the Transaqua Congo-transfer proposal is real
- **Mangrove lagoons** — blue carbon, erosion control, new ecosystems from surplus sand
- **Y Combinator's "Desert Flooding" Request for Startups** — flooding the Sahara for carbon sequestration was literally requested, in writing, by YC. We do all of the above.

No build, no dependencies. Three self-contained HTML files:

```bash
open index.html
```

Or serve it anywhere static files go (GitHub Pages works out of the box).
